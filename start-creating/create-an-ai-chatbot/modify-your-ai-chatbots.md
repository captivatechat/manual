# Modify your AI Chatbots

After you finish creating your AI Chatbot, you will return to the _**Setup > My AI Chatbots**_ view. Your newest AI Chatbot will be displayed as the first tile from the top left of your list.&#x20;

To maintain an existing AI Chatbot, select the tile from the list provided.  You can then proceed using any of the following steps:&#x20;

***

## Click the tile or the settings

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (9) (1).png" alt="Click onany AI Chatbot or the hamburger tile menu beside the AI Chatbot of your choice to access its settings. You can edit the AI Chatbot to go through the AI Chatbot creation process, make a copy, upload new data, do testing, or delete it entirely."><figcaption><p>Click onany AI Chatbot or the hamburger tile menu beside the AI Chatbot of your choice to access its settings. You can edit the AI Chatbot to go through the AI Chatbot creation process, make a copy, upload new data, do testing, or delete it entirely.</p></figcaption></figure>

You can modify your AI Chatbot by first **clicking any AI Chatbot tile or by clicking the settings (** ![Click the hamburger tile menu beside the AI Chatbot of your choice to access its settings. You can edit the AI Chatbot to go through the AI Chatbot creation process, make a copy, upload new data, do testing, or delete it entirely.](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (13) (1) (1).png>) **) ,** and then choosing an action you want accomplish:&#x20;

* **\*Edit:** Adjusts all parameters of the AI Chatbot.
* **Duplicate:** Creates a copy of the AI Chatbot.
* **Delete:** Permanently deletes the AI Chatbot.
* **\*Edit Information:** Imports relevant information to the Chatbot's database.
* **\*Edit Type:** Will send you to the Select Type page to choose or modify your selected AI Chatbot Type.&#x20;
* **\*Train:** Improves the AI Chatbot through training.
* **\*Select Integration:** Connects the Chatbot to another system.&#x20;

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Returning to&#x20;**_**+AI Chatbot**_

Options above with an asterisk (\*) (_**Edit, Add Information, Add Integration, Train**_**)** will return users back to their chosen AI Chatbot's respective _**+AI Chatbot**_ steps.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

***

## Click _Edit_

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (10) (1).png" alt="Click the &#x22;Edit&#x22; button either from the hamburger tile menu or on the lower right side of the &#x22;My AI Chatbots&#x22; window to edit specific parts of the AI Chatbot."><figcaption><p>Click the "Edit" button either from the hamburger tile menu or on the lower right side of the "My AI Chatbots" window to edit specific parts of the AI Chatbot.</p></figcaption></figure>

Choosing _**Edit**_ after **clicking settings (** ![Click the hamburger tile menu beside the AI Chatbot of your choice to access its settings. You can edit the AI Chatbot to go through the AI Chatbot creation process, make a copy, upload new data, do testing, or delete it entirely.](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (13) (1) (1).png>) **)** or ![Click the "Edit" button either from the hamburger tile menu or on the lower right side of the "My AI Chatbots" window to edit specific parts of the AI Chatbot.](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (16) (1) (1).png>) on the lower right-hand side of the screen.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Returning to&#x20;**_**+AI Chatbot**_

Both versions of _Edit_ will return users to **the beginning of the** _**+AI Chatbot**_ process.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

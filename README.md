## What is Captivate?

**Captivate** provides various AI solutions that transform the enterprise. As your AI development partner, we harness the power of AI alongside your processes, expertise, and data.&#x20;

Whether you're a digital leader aiming to unify your systems or a sales leader aiming to accelerate deal velocity, Captivate equips you with the tools to transform your sales delivery and leverage AI's incredible potential.

Captivate empowers your team through these main AI solutions:

* **Captivate Vienna:** Serving as our enterprise AI orchestration layer, Vienna connects, governs, and manages every AI agent, data source, and workflow across your organisation.
* **Captivate Athena:** The AI sales execution platform that supports reps before, during, and after every sales meeting with call prep, live prompts, and automated follow-up.&#x20;

Captivate is your go-to solution for bringing AI's power to your organisation.
